Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

The short version: blop works without an account and keeps your progress on your device. If you choose to sign in, your progress syncs through our database so it follows you between devices. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.

This policy explains what blop (“we”, “us”), the study app at bloplearn.space, collects, why, who it’s shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Data stored only on your device

Without an account, everything stays in your browser’s local storage: finished lessons, XP, streaks, review schedule, study plan, settings, job applications you log, mock interview drafts and results. We can’t see any of it. Clearing your browser data or using “Reset study progress” removes it.

2. Data we collect if you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google or with an email code, we receive:

  • Account details: your email address and an account ID. With Google sign-in, Google also shares your name and profile picture, which we don’t use or display.
  • Synced app data: the same data listed in section 1, so it can sync across your devices. This includes any company names, job titles and links you enter in the Jobs tab, and the answers you write in mock interviews.
  • Usage counts: how many mock interview answers you’ve had graded each day, used to enforce the daily limit.
  • Reminder settings: if you turn on reminders, your reminder time, time zone and a push subscription address for your device.

3. How we use it

  • To sync your progress between your devices.
  • To grade mock interview answers you submit.
  • To send the daily reminders you turn on. A reminder is only sent if you haven’t finished that day’s goal, and its text is based on your synced progress.
  • To prevent abuse, for example the limit of 20 graded answers per day.

We don’t use your data for advertising, and we don’t sell it or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

5. Cookies and tracking

blop doesn’t use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage to save your progress and, if you sign in, to keep you signed in. An offline cache (service worker) stores the app’s own files so it works without a connection.

6. How long we keep data

Synced data is kept until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. Daily grading counts are small records kept for abuse prevention. Reminder subscriptions are removed when you turn reminders off, when you choose “Sign out and clear this device”, or when your browser reports them as expired.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account: nothing leaves your device except what’s needed to load the app.
  • Turn things off: reminders can be switched off in Profile, and notifications can be blocked in your browser settings.
  • Delete your data: email us (section 10) to delete your account and all synced data. We’ll do so within 30 days.
  • Access or correct it: you can see and edit your data in the app, or ask us for a copy.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we’ll help.

8. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Database rules only let each signed-in user read and write their own data, and API keys stay on our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please don’t store anything in blop that you couldn’t afford to have exposed.

9. Children

blop is meant for people preparing for technical jobs and isn’t directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We don’t knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

Questions or deletion requests: blop@bloplearn.space
